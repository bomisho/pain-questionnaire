Tooth or Not: Facial Pain

العربية

Support

Tooth or Not: Facial Pain helps you understand what might be causing tooth or face pain, who to see and how soon. It does not diagnose and does not replace an examination by a dentist or doctor.

Email dr.tareq@gmail.com. Please do not include personal or health details.

If you have facial swelling that is spreading, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or any other emergency, call your local emergency number. Do not wait for a reply.

Subscription

  • Plans. A monthly or yearly subscription unlocks the app. New subscribers start with a free week.
  • Cancel or change plan. On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On a Mac: App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions. In the app you can also open Settings → Manage subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the free week or the current period ends.
  • New device. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and choose Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Refunds. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using the app

Tap Start a pain check. The first page asks about urgent warning signs; if any apply, the app tells you to get emergency care or to be seen the same day. Then answer the questions, mark where it hurts on the face and mouth map, try the optional self-checks, and see the possible causes with who to see and how soon. You can share a summary with your dentist or doctor. The language button switches between English and Arabic.

Your data

Your answers are not saved and never leave your device; they are cleared when you start a new check or close the app. See the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later.

استخدام التطبيق

اضغط «ابدأ فحص الألم». تسأل الصفحة الأولى عن علامات التحذير العاجلة؛ وإذا انطبق أيّ منها، يطلب منك التطبيق الحصول على رعاية طارئة أو المراجعة في اليوم نفسه. بعد ذلك أجب عن الأسئلة، وحدّد مكان الألم على خريطة الوجه والفم، وجرّب الفحوص الذاتية الاختيارية، ثم اطّلع على الأسباب المحتملة ومن تراجع ومتى. يمكنك مشاركة ملخص مع طبيب أسنانك أو طبيبك. يبدّل زر اللغة بين العربية والإنجليزية.
