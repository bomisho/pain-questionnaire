Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Tooth or Not: Facial Pain (shown on your device as “Facial pain”) helps people understand tooth and face pain. It is made by Tareq AlAli, an individual developer (“we”).

In short: the app collects no personal data. We run no servers, and there are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your answers stay on your device

Your answers about your pain are kept only in the app’s memory while you use it. They are not saved, and they are cleared when you start a new check or close the app. The app never sends them to us or to anyone else. It asks for no name, date of birth or contact details.

Sharing a summary

If you choose to share a summary with your dentist or doctor, it goes only where you send it, using your own messaging or email app.

Settings

The language you choose is stored on the device.

Subscriptions

Apple handles purchases, renewals and cancellations through the App Store. The app reads only Apple’s signed record of which plan is active and when the current period ends. We never receive names, email addresses or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply. Please do not include personal or health details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
